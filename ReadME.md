# Social Media Campaign Analytics

This Python project analyzes a simulated social media campaign for Urban Fashion.
The goal is to identify high-value customers by combining audience engagement,
sentiment, community influence, and geographic data.

## What I analyzed

- Ranked users by the likes, comments, and shares their posts received.
- Compared reach and post format to explore why some users received more interactions.
- Analyzed campaign sentiment, engagement by city, and connections within communities.

## Key finding

Zoe and Maya met all three criteria in the sample. Their posts received
above-median engagement, expressed positive sentiment, and came from users
with the most direct connections in their respective communities.

The dataset is synthetic and is defined inside the notebook.
